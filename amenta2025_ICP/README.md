# Reference:
Amenta, S., de Varda, A. G., Mandera, P., Keuleers, E., Brysbaert, M., & Marelli, M. (2024). The Italian Crowdsourcing Project: Visual word recognition times for 130,495 Italian words. Behavior Research Methods, 57(1), 26.

# Description:
- Instruction: Do you know this word?
- 1 out of 2 response options, "yes" and "no"
- 14908380 datapoints from 177111 individual participants from 148323 unique items
